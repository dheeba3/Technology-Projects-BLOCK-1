# Part 5 – Final Reflection
Write **200–300 words** describing your learning experience during this lab.
Consider:
* What surprised you about computer hardware?
* What components were new to you?
* What challenges did you face?
* What did you learn about embedded systems?
  
Throughout this lab, I learned a lot about computer hardware, the different components inside a computer and how they work together. Before completing this activity, I knew some basic computer parts, but I did not fully understand their functions. What surprised me most was how many different components are fitted inside a small computer, especially a MacBook. I also found it interesting that modern laptops have compact designs where many components are connected directly to the motherboard.

Some components that were new to me were the SoC (System on a Chip), GPIO pins and the Raspberry Pi microSD storage. I learned that the CPU processes instructions, RAM temporarily stores data, and the SSD stores files and applications. I also discovered that the Raspberry Pi uses GPIO pins to connect external sensors, motors and other electronic devices. This helped me understand how hardware can interact with the outside world.

One challenge I faced during this lab was identifying the internal components because some parts looked similar. Another challenge was understanding the different functions of the ports and connections on the Raspberry Pi. I overcame these challenges by looking at labelled diagrams, researching the components and comparing their functions. This made it easier to recognise and understand each part.

I also learned that embedded systems are computers designed to perform specific tasks. The Raspberry Pi is a good example because it combines several important components on one small circuit board. This makes it affordable, compact and energy efficient. I discovered that embedded systems are used in everyday technology, including smart homes, robotics, security systems and automatic devices.

Overall, this lab improved my knowledge of computer hardware and embedded systems. I gained practical knowledge of how components work together and developed more confidence in identifying hardware. I now understand the importance of each component and how computers are designed to perform different tasks efficiently.
